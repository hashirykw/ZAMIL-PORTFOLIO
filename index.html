About

Video editor, shooter and director in Karachi

I am Zamil Shaikh. I edit, shoot and direct video and manage social media, with a focus on content that looks professional and communicates clearly. I work from Karachi, Pakistan.

I co-founded Nexlyr Solutions, where I help lead creative production, video editing, social media work and client projects, and I founded Khidmat-e-Rizq, a food drive initiative supporting people through food distribution and community service. My work grew through hands-on projects with clients and businesses, where I learned to take an idea all the way to final delivery. Today I help businesses, brands and people turn their ideas into engaging videos, social content and a more professional online presence.
