How it runs

Each content cycle runs through the same four steps.

  • Understand the goal — Who the content is for, and what it needs to do once it is out there.
  • Agree the plan — The content, the scope and the deliverables, settled before any work starts.
  • Create and refine — Shooting and editing, with your feedback worked in along the way.
  • Deliver — Final files, ready to publish or use wherever they are going.
